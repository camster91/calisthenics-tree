Every rank, every tree.

Four calisthenics progressions, ten ranks each: 40 skills with the exact targets the app uses to unlock the next one. Placement starts you partway up, so you only train the rungs you need.

Targets are sets × reps or hold time. A Load tag means the move counts extra toward your strain score.

Vertical Push

Handstand push-up progression

Ten ranks from the wall push-up to a planche push-up on rings, through the pike push-up, the wall handstand and the freestanding handstand push-up. Bent-arm pushing first, then straight-arm shoulder work overhead.

  1. 1

    Rank 1: Wall push-up

    3 × 8 reps · bent-arm elbow, bent-arm shoulder

  2. 2

    Rank 2: Incline push-up

    3 × 8 reps · bent-arm elbow, bent-arm shoulder

  3. 3

    Rank 3: Standard push-up

    3 × 8 reps · bent-arm elbow, bent-arm shoulder

  4. 4

    Rank 4: Diamond push-up

    3 × 6 reps · bent-arm elbow, bent-arm shoulder

  5. 5

    Rank 5: Pike push-up

    3 × 6 reps · bent-arm elbow, bent-arm shoulder, wrist

  6. 6

    Rank 6: Wall handstand hold

    3 × 20 s hold · straight-arm shoulder, wrist

  7. 7

    Rank 7: Wall handstand push-up

    3 × 5 reps · straight-arm shoulder, wrist

  8. 8

    Rank 8: Freestanding handstand hold

    3 × 20 s hold · straight-arm shoulder, wrist

    Load ×1.8
  9. 9

    Rank 9: Freestanding handstand push-up

    3 × 3 reps · straight-arm shoulder, wrist

    Load ×1.8
  10. 10

    Rank 10: Planche push-up (rings)

    3 × 3 reps · straight-arm elbow, straight-arm shoulder, wrist

    Load ×1.8

When a set goes wrong

  • A freestanding handstand hold set held under 10 s drops you to the wall handstand hold. Balance, not strength, is usually what fails, so it skips the wall handstand push-up.

Horizontal Pull

Front lever progression

Ten ranks from a dead hang to the front lever pull, with your first pull-up at rank 5. Scapular strength and negatives build the pull-up; tuck, advanced tuck and straddle holds build the lever.

  1. 1

    Rank 1: Dead hang

    3 × 20 s hold · bent-arm shoulder, wrist

  2. 2

    Rank 2: Active hang

    3 × 15 s hold · bent-arm shoulder, lower back

  3. 3

    Rank 3: Scapular pulls

    3 × 8 reps · bent-arm shoulder, lower back

  4. 4

    Rank 4: Negative pull-up

    3 × 3 reps · bent-arm shoulder, bent-arm elbow, lower back

  5. 5

    Rank 5: Pull-up

    3 × 5 reps · bent-arm shoulder, bent-arm elbow, lower back

  6. 6

    Rank 6: Tuck front lever hold

    3 × 12 s hold · straight-arm shoulder, lower back

    Load ×1.8
  7. 7

    Rank 7: Advanced tuck front lever hold

    3 × 12 s hold · straight-arm shoulder, lower back

    Load ×1.8
  8. 8

    Rank 8: Straddle front lever hold

    3 × 10 s hold · straight-arm shoulder, lower back

    Load ×1.8
  9. 9

    Rank 9: Full front lever hold

    3 × 8 s hold · straight-arm shoulder, lower back

    Load ×1.8
  10. 10

    Rank 10: Front lever pull

    3 × 3 reps · straight-arm shoulder, straight-arm elbow, lower back

    Load ×1.8

When a set goes wrong

  • A tuck front lever hold set held under 8 s drops you to the dead hang first (or the active hang as the second choice) so the shoulder gets a full release from lever load.
  • The straddle front lever hold has a lateral link to the advanced tuck: a more compact lever for building straight-arm endurance with less load on the lower back.

Core

Dragon flag progression

Ten ranks from the plank to the full dragon flag, through the hollow body hold, the L-sit and toes-to-bar. Anti-extension bracing first, then compression, then the long lever.

  1. 1

    Rank 1: Plank

    3 × 20 s hold · lower back

  2. 2

    Rank 2: Side plank

    3 × 20 s hold · lower back

  3. 3

    Rank 3: Hollow body hold

    3 × 20 s hold · lower back, hip flexors

  4. 4

    Rank 4: L-sit (floor)

    3 × 15 s hold · lower back, hip flexors

  5. 5

    Rank 5: L-sit (parallel bars)

    3 × 12 s hold · lower back, hip flexors, bent-arm shoulder

  6. 6

    Rank 6: Hanging leg raise (toes-to-bar)

    3 × 6 reps · lower back, hip flexors, bent-arm shoulder

  7. 7

    Rank 7: Tuck dragon flag

    3 × 5 reps · lower back

  8. 8

    Rank 8: Advanced tuck dragon flag

    3 × 4 reps · lower back

  9. 9

    Rank 9: Straddle dragon flag

    3 × 3 reps · lower back

    Load ×1.8
  10. 10

    Rank 10: Full dragon flag

    3 × 3 reps · lower back

    Load ×1.8

When a set goes wrong

  • A hanging leg raise set under 3 reps drops you to the L-sit on parallel bars (or the floor L-sit as the second choice). Toes-to-bar usually fails on compression strength, and the L-sit builds exactly that.

Legs

Pistol squat progression

Ten ranks from the bodyweight squat to a heavy weighted pistol, through assisted pistols, Bulgarian split squats and the shrimp squat. Single-leg strength and balance, built one rung at a time.

  1. 1

    Rank 1: Bodyweight squat

    3 × 15 reps · hip flexors, lower back

  2. 2

    Rank 2: Box pistol squat (assisted)

    3 × 8 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

  3. 3

    Rank 3: Assisted pistol squat (counter)

    3 × 6 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

  4. 4

    Rank 4: Negative pistol squat

    3 × 5 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

    Load ×1.2
  5. 5

    Rank 5: Bulgarian split squat

    3 × 8 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

    Load ×1.2
  6. 6

    Rank 6: Shrimp squat

    3 × 5 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

    Load ×1.5
  7. 7

    Rank 7: Pistol squat (both legs parallel)

    3 × 5 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

    Load ×1.5
  8. 8

    Rank 8: Pistol squat (full)

    3 × 4 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

    Load ×1.8
  9. 9

    Rank 9: Weighted pistol squat (light)

    3 × 3 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

    Load ×2
  10. 10

    Rank 10: Weighted pistol squat (heavy)

    3 × 3 reps · hip flexors, lower back, wrist

    Load ×2.4

When a set goes wrong

  • A pistol squat (both legs parallel) set under 3 reps drops you to the shrimp squat.
  • A negative pistol squat set under 3 reps drops you back to the box pistol.
